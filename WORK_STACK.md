## Adaptive fluid performance

**Goal:** worthwhile automatic resolution for general gas/liquid mixtures, starting with CPU gas. Aim for at least 20% lower matched total simulation cost than the previous optimized uniform baseline on a large 1024² workload, at the user's agreed practical visual quality. Gains must survive evolving layouts. Desktop and browser are equally important for the eventual adaptive GPU engine. The overall goal is not yet established.

**Resume point:** the complete production CPU extraction is finished and faster than the upstream engine in the short 512² wave comparison. Agree the next gate: desktop/browser GPU work using this CPU reference, or the cloud-free 1024² CRASHING_WAVE evaluation against the older optimized uniform engine. Sustained liquid quality/performance remains unresolved. The conserved-update bottleneck proposal and original LOCAL/MOVING cloud depth investigation remain parked. Present native uniform is not the historical optimized-uniform baseline.

**Established foundation:** One maintained adaptive engine remains, entirely project-owned strictly 2D CPU code. AMReX/Hydro are optional reference-test dependencies only. Automatic selection, conservative regrid, cached ghost/face exchange, padding/history repair, and matching bounded-linear transport/interpolation/display are implemented. Fine/coarse decisions use actual state, without gravity, horizontal-cut, element-name or bubble special cases. Error-driven display detail is reconstructed, not secretly simulated at full resolution.

**Open limits:** mixed/fragmented layouts still need to earn their overhead; nearly uniform coarse execution alone does not prove useful adaptivity. Recent timings compare with the newer, more expensive uniform method and are not the historical performance gate. Local volume-closure drift with loose pressure persists despite global mass conservation (latest max 8 LOCAL peaks: 1.29% at 512², 0.105% at 1024² over 48 s); moving-detail trajectory error also remains. These are recorded limitations, not authorization for an unrelated solver rewrite.

**Completion / return:** when the agreed speed-and-appearance goal is demonstrated on representative evolving workloads, record the accepted scope and remaining limits, archive this entry, and return to the [broader fluid-world objective](#broader-fluid-world-objective). If this route is abandoned, retain its decisive finding before discussing the explicit ambient-reservoir alternative; do not silently switch physical models.

## Broader fluid-world objective

Free processing budget for a rich 2D environment in which organisms can evolve structures, membranes, organs and muscles, eventually coupled to deformable/rigid matter and phase changes. Elements/compounds may be abstract materials with temperature-dependent properties. This is direction, not a backlog to implement now.

**Return after the gas foundation:** agree the next integration milestone—richer liquid/mixture behavior, GPU migration, or isolated matter/phase-change experiments—using demonstrated cost and fidelity. Existing uniform liquid/GPU engines remain separate and working. A previously recorded GPU pure-material exactness failure reproduces outside AMReX and remains an independent unresolved issue; consult [foundation validation](.codex/runs/run0072_20261023_151735/final-validation.md) before GPU work. Do not claim those integrations are already validated.

**Alternative, not selected:** replacing eligible regions with a prescribed ambient reservoir is a different physical model. It remains available for discussion if adaptive resolution is insufficient; sleeping/ambient work must not silently discard gas or trapped bubbles.
